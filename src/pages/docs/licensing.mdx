# Licensing

LocalCloud is free to use during public preview under the **LocalCloud Public Preview License Agreement**.

<Callout type="info" title="Free public preview">
Individuals and organizations, including for-profit companies, may use LocalCloud free of charge for development, testing, ongoing internal CI, and evaluation. No payment method or license key is required.
</Callout>

LocalCloud is designed as a local development and testing environment. Release behavior should be validated against real Google Cloud before deploying an application to production. The preview grant does not cover customer-facing production use, reselling LocalCloud, offering it as a hosted or managed service, redistribution, or sublicensing.

## Resources

For engineering, security, and procurement teams reviewing LocalCloud for organizational use:

- **Governing License Agreement**: The complete legal terms are available in the <a href={governingLicenseUrl} target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">governing LICENSE agreement</a> in the runtime repository. The full license text controls.
- **Privacy and Outbound Data**: Detailed information regarding runtime telemetry, optional controls, and local data boundaries is documented in the [Privacy and Outbound Data Reference](/docs/privacy/).
- **Runtime Components**: The LocalCloud runtime, including its <a href={`${productFacts.runtimeRepositoryUrl}/blob/main/docs/MCP_INTEGRATION.md`}>built-in MCP integration</a> (accessible via the `/mcp` HTTP endpoint or the `localcloud mcp` stdio bridge), is covered under the same governing license.
- **Preview releases keep their terms**: Each version distributed under the Public Preview License remains available to its licensees under those terms. Later versions or services may be offered under different terms, but they do not retroactively alter the grant for preview releases already obtained under the agreement.
