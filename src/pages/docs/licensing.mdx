# Licensing reference

The governing **LocalCloud Community License Agreement** is a proprietary license issued by **LocalCloud Inc.** The <a href={governingLicenseUrl}>complete license agreement</a> controls; this page summarizes the current terms.

<Callout type="warning" title="Technical availability is not legal permission">
A service starting successfully, a development image exposing Pro-tier behavior, or documentation showing a workflow does not grant permission beyond the governing agreement.
</Callout>

## Community License: free

The Community License grants royalty-free Community Use to exactly these eligible groups:

- **Individual developers** acting personally for learning, experimentation, and personal projects;
- **Students** acting personally or academically for coursework, teaching, research, and skill development; and
- **Nonprofit organizations** legally organized and operated as nonprofits for internal operations, development, testing, education, research, and mission-related programs, including production use supporting the nonprofit mission.

Community eligibility does not cover work performed for a for-profit employer, client, or business. A person using LocalCloud for their own for-profit business is also acting for a For-Profit Entity under the agreement.

## Commercial License: contact us

A for-profit company requires a separate written Commercial License before any use by, on behalf of, or for its benefit. This includes commercial product development, internal business systems, employee workflows, team CI, business cost reduction, and paid consulting or contracting.

Contact [data.oculus.llc@gmail.com](mailto:data.oculus.llc@gmail.com?subject=LocalCloud%20commercial%20licensing%20inquiry) and describe the company and planned use. An inquiry, negotiation, quote, invoice, or payment does not grant commercial rights until LocalCloud Inc. and the licensee execute a Commercial License.

The Community License does not grant rights to an organization outside its defined eligible groups. Contact LocalCloud for a separate written agreement if your organization is neither a qualifying nonprofit nor a for-profit company.

## Technical enforcement

| Runtime condition | Technical behavior | Legal boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Development image with enforcement disabled | May expose technical Pro access without an API key. | Does not expand the license grant. |
| Enforced/production build | Requires `LOCALCLOUD_API_KEY`; validation failure terminates startup. | A technically accepted key does not replace a required written agreement. |
| Service `minTier` | Controls technical service availability. | Community/Pro labels are access controls, not the two pricing categories. |

## License metadata conflicts

Some historical package and image metadata previously declared `Apache-2.0`, including a runtime OCI label. Those declarations conflict with the governing proprietary root license and must not be used to infer LocalCloud runtime permissions.

The LocalCloud runtime, including its <a href={`${productFacts.runtimeRepositoryUrl}/blob/main/docs/MCP_INTEGRATION.md`}>built-in MCP integration</a>, remains governed by the runtime repository license. Technical access through the canonical `/mcp` endpoint or the `localcloud mcp` stdio bridge does not create a separate grant.

## Before using LocalCloud

1. Read the <a href={governingLicenseUrl}>complete governing license</a>.
2. Identify whether you qualify as an Individual Developer, Student, or Nonprofit Organization.
3. If the use is by or for a for-profit company, obtain a separate Commercial License before use.
4. Re-check the exact license shipped with the image or release you plan to use.

For questions about the agreement or commercial licensing, contact [data.oculus.llc@gmail.com](mailto:data.oculus.llc@gmail.com?subject=LocalCloud%20commercial%20licensing%20inquiry).
