<p>{freeUsePolicy.continuity} This is an ongoing free-use policy, independent of the limited-time preview.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Individual developers:</strong> local, non-production development and testing, including personal, private, freelance, and work projects, plus CI for their own projects.</li>
  <li><strong>Open-source projects:</strong> development, testing, contributor fork checks, and ongoing internal CI for projects distributed under an open-source license, including company-sponsored projects.</li>
  <li><strong>Other organizational use:</strong> shared CI and team-wide use for proprietary projects follow the terms supplied with the release. They are currently free under the public preview.</li>
</ul>

<p>Tests against local endpoints do not need a maintainer's Google Cloud project or incur Google Cloud service charges for those operations. Local compute, storage, and CI runner costs still apply. Repositories can include configuration and setup instructions that direct contributors to obtain the runtime themselves.</p>

Existing preview releases retain their granted terms. This website update does not replace a license supplied with an earlier release.</p>

## Limited-time public preview

LocalCloud is free to use during the limited-time public preview under the **LocalCloud Public Preview License Agreement**.

<Callout type="info" title="Free public preview">
Individuals and organizations, including for-profit companies, may use LocalCloud free of charge for development, testing, ongoing internal CI, and evaluation. No payment method or license key is required.
</Callout>

LocalCloud is designed for development and testing. Validate release behavior against real Google Cloud before deploying an application to production. The preview grant excludes customer-facing production use, resale, hosting as a service, redistribution, and sublicensing.
